# SupplyChainIQ — Business Recommendations

## 1. Executive Summary

The analysis of the DataCo Smart Supply Chain dataset identified significant delivery-performance challenges, differences in shipping-mode reliability, regional delivery risks, and variations in product-category and customer sales contribution.

The following recommendations translate these findings into actionable operational and business strategies.

## 2. Key Findings

- **Overall late-delivery rate:** 54.83% (98,977 out of 180,519 order items).
- **First Class:** 95.32% late-delivery risk, with an average shipping delay of approximately 1 day.
- **Second Class:** 76.63% late-delivery risk, with an average delay of approximately 1.99 days.
- **Same Day:** 45.74% late-delivery risk, with an average delay of approximately 0.48 days.
- **Standard Class:** 38.07% late-delivery risk, with an average delay close to zero.
- **Total sales:** 36.78 million (dataset currency unspecified).

## 3. Data-Driven Business Recommendations

### 3.1 Optimize First Class Shipping Performance

First Class exhibits the highest late-delivery risk among the shipping modes.

**Recommendations:**
- Investigate carrier reliability and dispatch bottlenecks.
- Review delivery commitments and estimated delivery timelines.
- Monitor First Class performance through regular operational reviews.

### 3.2 Improve Second Class Delivery Reliability

Second Class has the highest average shipping delay at approximately 1.99 days.

**Recommendations:**
- Examine warehouse processing and order-handling delays.
- Review transportation routes and carrier handoffs.
- Evaluate alternative shipping assignments where operationally feasible.

### 3.3 Reduce Overall Late Deliveries

The overall late-delivery rate of 54.83% indicates a substantial delivery-performance challenge.

**Recommendations:**
- Establish delivery-performance monitoring by shipping mode and region.
- Identify recurring causes of late deliveries.
- Introduce alerts for consistently high-risk operational segments.

### 3.4 Strengthen Regional Logistics Management

Regional delivery analysis can help identify locations with elevated late-delivery risk.

**Recommendations:**
- Prioritize high-risk regions for operational investigation.
- Evaluate regional transportation constraints and fulfillment capacity.
- Monitor carrier performance across different regions.

### 3.5 Improve Inventory and Demand Planning

Sales trends and product-category performance provide insights into demand patterns.

**Recommendations:**
- Use monthly sales trends to support demand forecasting.
- Prioritize inventory availability for high-performing product categories.
- Align replenishment and warehouse capacity with observed demand patterns.

### 3.6 Focus on Customer Retention

Customer-segment contribution and top-customer sales analysis can support customer-focused decisions.

**Recommendations:**
- Monitor fulfillment performance for high-value customers.
- Identify customer segments contributing significantly to sales.
- Use delivery reliability and sales trends to support retention strategies.

## 4. Expected Business Impact

Implementing these recommendations could support:

- Improved delivery reliability and shipping performance.
- Better identification of operational bottlenecks.
- More informed shipping-mode and logistics decisions.
- Improved inventory planning and product availability.
- Stronger customer retention and service quality.
- Data-driven operational decision-making.

| Priority | Focus Area | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|
| High | First Class shipping | Investigate exceptionally high late-delivery risk |
| High | Second Class shipping | Address the highest average shipping delay |
| High | Overall delivery | Monitor and investigate late-delivery patterns |

## 6. Conclusion

SupplyChainIQ demonstrates how Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI can be used together to analyze supply chain operations and translate business data into actionable recommendations.

**Note:** These recommendations are based on historical data from 2015 to January 2018. They represent analytical suggestions rather than measured operational improvements. Current business conditions should be validated before implementation.
